Crisis support information

One Step is a coursework prototype, not a crisis service. It is not monitored. No one is reviewing messages or providing professional care.

In the United States, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the United States).

Outside the United States, use local emergency or crisis services.
